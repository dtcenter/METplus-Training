Tutorial Survey
===============

**METplus Tutorial Survey**

Thank you for your time and attention. We really enjoyed teaching you about METplus.

.. note::

   Please take some time to fill out the `METplus Tutorial Survey <https://forms.gle/xXuUagPgU5J7PcK6A>`_ to suggest improvements!

If you have more questions in your use of METplus, please:

* Refer to the `MET <https://metplus.readthedocs.io/projects/met/en/main_v11.1/Users_Guide/index.html>`_ and `METplus <https://metplus.readthedocs.io/en/main_v5.1/Users_Guide/index.html>`_ Documentation.
* Refer to the `MET Online Tutorial <https://dtcenter.org/community-code/model-evaluation-tools-met/training>`_.
* Refer to the `Known Issues Section <https://dtcenter.org/community-code/model-evaluation-tools-met/download>`_ for each release.
* Use a search engine to search for met_help followed by your question, issue, or error message.
* Browse through the `MET-Help email archive <http://mailman.ucar.edu/pipermail/met_help>`_.
* Open a `Discussions <https://github.com/dtcenter/METplus/discussions>`_ question for help from METplus staff and community.

**Now go out there and verify!**
